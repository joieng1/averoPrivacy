# **Privacy Policy – Version 2.1.0**

**Avero App**

*Last updated: October 28, 2025*

---

## **1. Overview**

Avero is a productivity app that helps users stay focused by completing verified tasks.
We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how your data is collected, used, and protected.

---

## **2. Information We Collect**

### **a. User Content**

* **Tasks and Descriptions:** When you create or complete a task, we store the task title, description, and completion status.
* **Photos or Proof Images:** If you choose to upload proof of task completion (such as a photo), it will be stored securely until you delete it.
* **Peer Verification:** If you select peer verification, your chosen friends may view your proof submissions solely for verification purposes.

### **b. Identifiers**

* **Account Information:** We use Firebase Authentication to create and manage user accounts. This includes your email address and a unique user ID.
* **Device Identifiers:** Firebase may assign a unique identifier to your device for login, analytics, and crash reporting.

### **c. Usage Data (Optional Analytics)**

* We may collect information about how you interact with the app, such as when you complete tasks, upload photos, or open certain screens.
* This data helps us improve app performance and user experience.

### **d. Diagnostics**

* We use Firebase Crashlytics and/or Sentry to collect crash and performance data.
* This includes non-personal information like device type, app version, and error logs.

---

## **3. How We Use Your Data**

Your data is used only to:

1. Provide the core functionality of Avero (e.g., saving tasks and verifying completion).
2. Improve app performance and reliability.
3. Communicate important updates, if applicable.

We **do not** sell your data or use it for advertising or marketing purposes.

---

## **4. Data Storage and Retention**

* Task and photo data are stored in **Google Firebase** (Firestore and Storage).
* You may delete your account or individual tasks/photos at any time, and your data will be permanently removed.
* Backups may persist for a limited time for security and recovery purposes.

---

## **5. Data Sharing**

We **do not share** your personal data with third parties except:

* When required by law or valid legal request.
* When you explicitly enable peer verification (limited access to selected friends).
* For internal services such as Firebase and Google Cloud, which process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality and security standards.
